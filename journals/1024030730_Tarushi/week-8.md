# Week 8: Testing, Settings and Final Improvements

## Objective
The objective was to complete the remaining screens, test the full application and make final improvements to prepare the project for submission.

## Work Completed
- Completed the Statistics screen with a summary of alerts and monitoring time across sessions.
- Developed the Settings screen so that the alert sound can be turned on or off and the sensitivity can be adjusted.
- Tested the complete system with different users, lighting conditions and head positions.
- Checked the detection behaviour for normal blinking, eye closure, talking and yawning.
- Adjusted the detection flow and reduced false alerts based on the test results.
- Fixed small interface and navigation issues across the application screens.
- Prepared the final documentation and summary of the project work.

## Challenges / Observations
Detection was less reliable in low light and when the face was turned away from the camera. Normal talking could sometimes be mistaken for mouth opening, so the consecutive-frame condition was important for avoiding false alerts.

## Learning
Learned the importance of testing in real conditions and not only during development. Also learned how small adjustments to thresholds and settings can noticeably improve the reliability and usability of an application.

## Week 8 Outcome
The Driver Drowsiness Detection application was completed with camera-based monitoring, EAR/MAR detection, alerts, history, statistics and settings. The project was tested and is ready for final submission. Further improvements could include better performance in low light and support for additional alert options.
