# Week 2: EAR and MAR Logic

## Objective
The objective was to understand and develop the logic for detecting eye closure and yawning.

## Work Completed
- Studied the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) for measuring eye openness.
- Studied the Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) for identifying mouth opening.
- Explored how thresholds could be used to classify eye closure and possible yawning.
- Worked on the initial logic for detecting these conditions.

## Challenges / Observations
Choosing suitable thresholds and understanding how to distinguish normal movements from possible drowsiness were important considerations.

## Learning
Learned how facial measurements can be used to identify changes in eye and mouth movements.

## Week 2 Outcome
The basic EAR and MAR detection approach was developed and understood.
