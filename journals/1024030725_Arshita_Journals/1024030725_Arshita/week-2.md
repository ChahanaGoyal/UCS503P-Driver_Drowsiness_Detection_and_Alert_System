# Week 2: EAR and MAR Logic

## Objective
The focus was to understand and build the initial logic for identifying closed eyes and yawning.

## Work Completed
- Studied how the Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) measures how open the eyes are.
- Examined the Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) as a measure of mouth opening.
- Explored the use of thresholds to recognise eye closure and possible yawning.
- Developed the initial logic for identifying these conditions.

## Challenges / Observations
Selecting appropriate thresholds and separating normal facial movements from possible signs of drowsiness required careful consideration.

## Learning
Understood how facial measurements help track changes in eye and mouth movements.

## Week 2 Outcome
The basic detection approach using EAR and MAR was understood and developed.
