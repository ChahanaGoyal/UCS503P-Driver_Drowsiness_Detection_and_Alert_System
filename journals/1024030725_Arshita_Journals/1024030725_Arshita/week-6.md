# Week 6: EAR/MAR Integration and Drowsiness Detection

## Objective
The goal was to integrate facial measurements and detection logic into the Android monitoring application.

## Work Completed
- Worked on showing the calculated EAR and MAR readings on the monitoring screen.
- Integrated the eye-closure detection logic into the monitoring workflow.
- Worked on drowsiness detection using the chosen EAR threshold and consecutive-frame requirement.
- Checked the monitoring interface and the way detection status is displayed.
- Identified the work still needed to bring all application components together.

## Challenges / Observations
The key challenge remains making the camera, facial measurements, detection logic and interface operate consistently as one system.

## Learning
Learned about combining detection logic with a mobile app and why the complete system needs testing alongside its individual components.

## Week 6 Outcome
The app has reached camera-based monitoring with EAR/MAR readings and drowsiness detection available. Complete integration, alerts, dashboard development and additional testing are still pending.
